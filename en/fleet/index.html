The right vehicle for every shipment

Our fleet ranges from the compact Citan to the Sprinter Box with tail lift. The right vehicle for your shipment depends on dimensions, weight and type of loading. We choose it with you when you make your request.

Every vehicle carries a GPS tracker, so our dispatch team can see it at all times. Your load is insured for the entire journey, and our drivers are trained.

For heavy goods we also send two drivers. Find out more under Two-person delivery.

Questions about our fleet.

How much can a vehicle carry?

Each run carries up to 1.37 tonnes. The payload depends on the model and is listed for each vehicle on this page.

Which vehicle has a tail lift?

The Mercedes Sprinter Box has a tail lift. It loads from the rear only.

Can I choose the vehicle myself?

Tell us the dimensions and weight, and we will choose the right vehicle and name it in the quote.

Can the vehicles be tracked?

Yes. With GPS trackers, our dispatch team can see every vehicle.
