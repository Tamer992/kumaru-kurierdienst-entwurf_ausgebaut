Prepare shipments, avoid mistakes

A shipment needs more than a box. It has to be registered, carry the right label and travel with the right documents. If a detail is missing, the shipment gets stuck at customs or with the recipient. We take care of this work for you.

You hand over your parcels ready packed, with delivery note and invoice. If a shipment goes to a country outside the EU, the customs documents are enclosed. We register every shipment, check the documents for completeness and apply the right label to the parcel. For customers in the chemical industry, we also check the dangerous goods shipments. A lorry then collects the finished shipments from us.

You can combine the shipping service with our runs: direct runs, express runs, runs to other European countries and dangerous goods transport.

What we handle for your shipping.

  • Registering shipments

    We fully register every parcel you hand over to us.

  • Checking documents

    We check the delivery note and invoice for completeness before the shipment moves on.

  • Customs documents outside the EU

    If a shipment goes to a country outside the EU, we check that the customs documents are complete and enclosed.

  • Applying labels

    Every shipment gets the right label, clearly visible and legible.

  • Checking for dangerous goods

Questions about the shipping service.

Do you store goods?

No. You hand over your parcels ready packed. We register every shipment, check the documents for completeness and apply the right label.

Which documents do you need?

A delivery note and invoice for every shipment. For shipments to countries outside the EU, the customs documents as well. We check that everything is complete.

Do I need to pack the parcels myself?

Yes. You hand over the parcels ready packed. We take care of registration, document checks and labels.

What happens after labelling?

A lorry collects the finished shipments from us and takes them away.

Do you also check dangerous goods?

Yes. For customers in the chemical industry, we check dangerous goods shipments for marking and accompanying documents before the lorry collects them.
