Two drivers instead of one

Some shipments need more hands. Heavy machine parts, pallets and bulky goods cannot be moved safely by one person. With two-person delivery, two drivers arrive, load together and carry the goods to the agreed place at the destination.

The right vehicle depends on dimensions and weight. Each run carries up to 1.37 tonnes. The Sprinter Box with tail lift offers a load length of 437 cm, a load width of 211 cm and a load height of 211 cm. You will find every model on our Fleet.

In your request, give us the weight, dimensions and the route to the drop-off point, such as floor or loading bay. This lets us plan the vehicle, crew and time correctly and send you a no-obligation quote.

Questions about two-person delivery.

When is two-person delivery worthwhile?

Whenever your goods are too heavy or too bulky for one person, for example pallets, machine parts and large equipment.

What weight is possible?

Each run carries up to 1.37 tonnes. The payload depends on the vehicle and is listed on our Fleet.

Do you have vehicles with a tail lift?

Yes, the Sprinter Box has a tail lift. It loads from the rear only.

Do the drivers carry the goods inside?

The drivers bring the goods to the place you agree with us in your request. Tell us the floor, loading bay and access.

Is the run insured?

Yes. Your load is insured for the entire journey.
