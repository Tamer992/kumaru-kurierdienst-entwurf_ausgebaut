Storage close by, delivery by our own drivers

If you deliver to many recipients, you need storage close by and shipping that follows your business. At Kumaru, your goods are stored in Brühl and our own drivers deliver them from there. There is no handover to third parties and no parcel network in between.

You tell us what and how much you want to store, and which shipment goes to whom and when. We store the goods, pick and pack the shipments and deliver at the time you set. We discuss type and quantity before we start, so the quote fits your needs.

It combines with all our other services: express runs, personal handover, Two-person delivery and runs to other European countries.

Questions about warehousing and fulfilment.

Do you also store goods?

Yes. We store your goods and ship them to your instructions. Tell us the type and quantity and we will come back to you with a quote.

Who delivers the goods?

Our own drivers handle delivery, in Germany and across Europe.

What goods can I store?

Merchandise, spare parts, textiles and electronics are among the most common. For special goods, talk to us before storage.

How do I request a quote?

Use our Request a quote page or call us. You will receive the no-obligation quote as soon as possible.

Do you deliver to PO boxes?

No. Every delivery needs a business address.
