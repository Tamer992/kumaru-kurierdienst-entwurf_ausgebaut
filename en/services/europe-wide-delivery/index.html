One driver from Brühl to the destination country

From the Rhine-Neckar metropolitan region, we take your shipment to the recipient abroad without reloading. Your goods stay in one vehicle and leave it only at the destination.

We offer road delivery to Germany, Austria, Belgium, the Czech Republic, Switzerland, Denmark, Spain, Finland, France, Croatia, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, the United Kingdom, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Sweden, Slovakia, Slovenia and many more. If your destination country is not on the list, ask us.

On a cross-trade run, we collect your shipment at one location and deliver it to another, without a detour via Brühl. We agree the necessary accompanying documents for non-EU countries with you before the run.

Questions about Europe-wide delivery.

Which countries do you deliver to?

We deliver to over 25 European countries, including Austria, Belgium, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland, Switzerland, Spain and the United Kingdom. If your destination country is missing, ask us.

How long does an international delivery take?

It depends on the route, border crossings and traffic. We give you the expected journey time with the quote.

What is a cross-trade run?

We collect your shipment at one location and deliver it to another. The route does not pass through our base in Brühl.

Is my shipment insured abroad?

Yes. Your load is insured for the entire journey.

Do you deliver to private addresses?

No. Every delivery needs a business address.
