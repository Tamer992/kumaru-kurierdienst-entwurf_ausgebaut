From express transport to warehousing

Kumaru Kurierdienst transports parcels and urgent shipments, documents, merchandise, spare parts, medicines, blood products, files, textiles, electronics and dangerous goods. The right service for your shipment depends on the deadline, destination and type of goods.

Every service rests on the same foundation: one driver carries your shipment without reloading, your load is insured, and the recipient confirms receipt with a signature. We are owner-managed, independent and have been in business since 2011.

Not sure which service is right? Call us. We will advise you and send a no-obligation quote within minutes.

What always applies at Kumaru.

Whatever the service, these principles stay the same.

  • Personal contact

    When you call, you speak to the people who plan your shipment.

  • Fast collection

    Our drivers collect your goods at the agreed time.

  • Transit insurance

    Your load is insured for the entire journey.

  • Proof of delivery

    The recipient confirms receipt with a signature.

  • Live tracking

    With GPS trackers, our dispatch team can see every vehicle.

  • Trained staff

    Our drivers are trained and hold the ADR certificate for dangerous goods.

How an order works.

  • Send request

    Call us or use the form. We need the collection point, destination and shipment details.

  • Receive your quote

    Within minutes we come back to you with a no-obligation quote.

  • Transport begins

    Once you place the order, we collect immediately and drive straight to the destination.
