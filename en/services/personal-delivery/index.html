Your shipment reaches the right hands

Some shipments must never be left at the door, at reception or with a neighbour. With personal handover, the driver gives the goods to the person you name as recipient. This applies to confidential documents and to goods that must not be left unattended.

The recipient confirms receipt with a signature, so you know when and to whom your shipment was handed over. The run itself is direct with no reloading, and one driver stays with your goods from collection to handover.

In your request, give us the recipient's name, business address and the times they are available. Every delivery needs a business address, and we do not deliver to PO boxes.

Questions about personal handover.

What does personal handover mean?

The driver hands the shipment directly to the recipient you name, who signs for receipt.

Is the shipment left without a signature?

With personal handover, the driver hands over only to the named recipient. The recipient confirms receipt with a signature.

Do you deliver to private addresses or PO boxes?

No. Every delivery needs a business address.

Can I combine personal handover with an express run?

Yes. See Same-day and express and mention both requirements in your request.

Is the shipment insured?

Yes. Your load is insured for the entire journey.
