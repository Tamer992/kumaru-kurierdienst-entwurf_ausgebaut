When care matters

Some shipments cannot take detours. Medicines and blood products are time-critical, confidential files must reach one person only, and dangerous goods need drivers who know the regulations. At Kumaru, a trained driver takes your shipment straight to the recipient.

Our drivers hold the ADR certificate for transporting dangerous goods and comply with all regulations. We also carry fireworks. Talk to us before you plan, so we can clarify the type, quantity and packaging of your goods.

The recipient confirms receipt with a signature. Your load is insured for the entire journey. To find out how handover to a named person works, see Personal handover.

Questions about sensitive and dangerous goods.

Which dangerous goods do you transport?

We clarify this case by case before every run. Tell us the substance, quantity and packaging and we will tell you how we will carry the shipment.

Do your drivers hold the ADR certificate?

Yes. Our drivers are trained and hold the ADR certificate for dangerous goods.

How do you transport medicines and blood products?

A driver collects the shipment and takes it straight to the recipient within a few hours. For special requirements, talk to us before the run.

Are confidential files safe with you?

One driver takes the files straight to the recipient, with no reloading and no third-party freight in the vehicle. The recipient signs for receipt.

Is the run insured?

Yes. Every shipment is insured.
