When the shipment has to arrive today

Express runs start as soon as your request is confirmed. A driver collects the goods and takes them straight to the recipient, with no collection stops and no reloading. Delivery takes place within a few hours, depending on destination, traffic and the size of the shipment.

Same-day runs need to be agreed in advance. Tell us the collection point, destination and the time the goods must arrive. We will tell you straight away whether the run is possible for that time and send a no-obligation quote within minutes.

Our dispatch team sees every vehicle via GPS and responds immediately to delays. You can reach us by phone Monday to Friday from 07:00 to 18:00 and on Saturdays from 09:00 to 18:00 (German time). We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Questions about same-day and express runs.

How fast is an express run?

Delivery takes place within a few hours. The exact time depends on the destination, traffic and the size of the shipment.

How do I book an express run?

Fastest by phone on +49 (0)6202 409 6491. You can also use the Request a quote page and we will reply as soon as possible.

How late can I book a same-day run?

Call us during our opening hours. We will tell you straight away whether the run is possible today.

Is an express run insured?

Yes. Every run is insured.

Which shipments suit express runs?

Parcels and urgent shipments, documents, merchandise, critical spare parts, medicines, blood products and confidential files.
