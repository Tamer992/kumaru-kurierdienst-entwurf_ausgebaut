A direct run instead of a parcel network

On a direct run, the vehicle belongs to your shipment for the duration of the order. The driver loads at the collection point, takes the shortest sensible route and hands over the goods at the destination. There are no transfer hubs, collection points or waiting times at a distribution centre.

This protects delicate goods because they are moved less often. It saves time, because the journey time is the pure distance from collection to destination. We drive from Brühl in the Rhine-Neckar metropolitan region, for example as your Courier in Mannheim, throughout Germany and to over 25 European countries.

When you call, you speak to the people who plan your run. The recipient confirms receipt with a signature, and your load is insured for the entire journey.

Questions about direct transport.

How is a direct run different from parcel shipping?

On a direct run, one driver takes your shipment alone from the collection point to the destination. With parcel shipping, goods pass through distribution centres and are reloaded several times.

How fast is a direct run?

Delivery takes place within a few hours. The exact time depends on the destination, traffic and the size of the shipment.

What weight can I send?

Each run carries up to 1.37 tonnes. To see which vehicle suits your shipment, visit our Fleet.

How do I book a direct run?

Call us or send the form on our Request a quote page. You will receive a no-obligation quote by email as soon as possible.

Do you deliver to PO boxes?

No. Every delivery needs a business address.
