A transport company from Brühl.

Kumaru Kurierdienst is an owner-managed transport company in Brühl, right next to Mannheim in Germany. Since 2011 we have carried shipments for companies in industry, retail and logistics: in the region, throughout Germany and to over 25 European countries.

No corporate group dictates our processes. We see our customers as partners and develop processes with them that fit their business.

On the phone, you speak to the people who plan your shipment. That applies to an urgent one-off run as much as to regular routes.

What you can rely on.

These principles apply to every run, every industry and every vehicle.

  • Direct run

    One driver collects and drives to the recipient without reloading.

  • Transit insurance

    Your load is insured for the entire journey.

  • Proof of delivery

    The recipient confirms receipt with a signature.

  • Trained drivers

    Our drivers are trained and hold the ADR certificate for dangerous goods.

  • GPS tracking

    With GPS trackers, our dispatch team can see every vehicle.

  • Personal contact

    You speak directly to the people who plan your run.
