One base, three ranges

Kumaru Kurierdienst operates from Brühl, right next to Mannheim. From there our drivers set off into the Rhine-Neckar metropolitan region and the Palatinate, across Germany and to over 25 European countries.

The same principle applies on every route: one driver collects your shipment and takes it to the recipient without reloading. Your load is insured, and the recipient confirms receipt with a signature.

Not shipping from Brühl? On a cross-trade run we collect your goods from any location and deliver them straight to the destination.

What applies on every route.

Mannheim or Madrid, these principles stay the same.

  • One driver

    Your goods stay in the same vehicle from collection to destination.

  • Transit insurance

    Your load is insured for the entire journey.

  • Live tracking

    With GPS trackers, our dispatch team can see every vehicle.

  • Proof of delivery

    The recipient confirms receipt with a signature.

  • Trained drivers

    Our drivers hold the ADR certificate for dangerous goods.

  • Personal contact

    On the phone, you speak to the people who plan your run.

Questions about our coverage.

Where does Kumaru Kurierdienst deliver?

From Brühl we serve the Rhine-Neckar metropolitan region and the Palatinate, the whole of Germany and over 25 European countries. If your destination country is not on the list, ask us.

Do you also run between two other locations?

Yes. On a cross-trade run we collect the shipment at one location and take it straight to another, without a detour via Brühl. More under Europe-wide delivery.

How quickly can you reach Mannheim or Heidelberg?

Brühl is right next to Mannheim, so our drivers reach the towns in the region quickly. For urgent shipments, use our Same-day and express.

Does the coverage apply to every service?

Yes. We offer direct transport, same-day, dangerous goods, personal handover and two-person delivery in the region, in Germany and in Europe. The details are listed under Services.

How do I find out whether my destination is possible?

Give us the collection point and destination by phone or through our Request a quote page. You will receive a no-obligation quote as soon as possible.
