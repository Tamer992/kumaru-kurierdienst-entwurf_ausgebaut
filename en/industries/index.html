Healthcare and pharma

Medicines and blood products cannot wait for detours. A driver collects the shipment and takes it straight to the recipient without reloading. For urgent cases we deliver same-day.

The driver hands over the shipment in person and has the receipt signed. Every run is insured.

Typical shipments
Medicines, blood products and urgent shipments between surgeries, pharmacies and hospitals.
Matching services
  • Same-day and express
  • Sensitive and dangerous goods
  • Personal handover

Industry and spare parts

When a plant stands still, every hour counts. We collect the spare part from the manufacturer or dealer and take it straight to your production site, in the region, in Germany and in over 25 European countries.

Two drivers deliver heavy machine parts and pallets. Each run carries up to 1.37 tonnes.

Typical shipments
Critical spare parts, machine parts and pallets.
Matching services
  • Direct transport
  • Same-day and express
  • Two-person delivery

Chemicals and dangerous goods

At Kumaru, only ADR-certified drivers carry dangerous goods. They are trained for dangerous goods and comply with all regulations.

Talk to us before you plan. We will clarify the substance, quantity and destination and plan the run to match.

Typical shipments
Dangerous goods under ADR, including fireworks.
Matching services
  • Sensitive and dangerous goods
  • Direct transport
  • Europe-wide delivery

Law firms and administration

Contracts, files and originals belong in the right hands. The driver hands the documents to the recipient in person, who signs for receipt.

Confidential files stay in the same vehicle from collection to destination. Nobody reloads them and nobody sorts them on the way.

Typical shipments
Confidential files, contracts, originals and urgent documents.
Matching services
  • Personal handover
  • Same-day and express
  • Direct transport

Retail and textiles

We store your goods and ship them to your customers according to your instructions. Our own drivers handle delivery, in Germany and across Europe.

From single parcels to pallets, we carry merchandise, textiles and electronics. Two drivers bring bulky goods to the recipient.

Typical shipments
Merchandise, textiles, electronics, parcels and pallets.

Questions about industries.

Which industries does Kumaru Kurierdienst serve?

Healthcare and pharma, industry, chemicals, law firms and administration, and retail and textiles. Your industry is not listed? Call us and we will look at your shipment.

Do you transport dangerous goods?

Yes. Our drivers hold the ADR certificate and comply with all regulations. More under Sensitive and dangerous goods.

How fast does a spare part reach the recipient?

Delivery takes place within a few hours. The exact time depends on the destination, traffic and the size of the shipment.

Who receives confidential documents?

The driver hands them to the recipient in person, who signs for receipt. We do not deliver to PO boxes and need a business address.

Tell us the collection point, destination and type of shipment. The no-obligation quote comes by email as soon as possible.
