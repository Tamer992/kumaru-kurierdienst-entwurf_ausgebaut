Shipment and process

How quickly can I expect a courier delivery?

Delivery takes place within a few hours. The exact time depends on the destination, traffic and the size of the shipment.

How do I book a transport?

Call us or send the form on our Request a quote page. You will receive a no-obligation quote by email as soon as possible.

Is there a maximum weight?

Each run carries up to 1.37 tonnes. The payload depends on the vehicle, and you can see every model on our Fleet.

How safe is my shipment?

Experienced drivers with the right equipment transport your shipment. Every run is insured.

What we carry

What does Kumaru transport?

Parcels and urgent shipments, documents, merchandise, critical spare parts, blood products, medicines, confidential files, textiles and electronics. Dangerous goods and fireworks too.

How does a dangerous goods run work?

Our drivers are trained for dangerous goods and comply with all regulations. Talk to us before you plan. More under Dangerous and sensitive goods.

Do you also store goods?

Yes. We store your goods and ship them to your instructions. Tell us the type and quantity and we will come back to you with a quote. Details under Warehousing and fulfilment.

Delivery and coverage

Which countries do you deliver to?

We deliver to over 25 European countries without reloading, including Austria, Belgium, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland, Switzerland and Spain. If your destination country is missing, ask us. More under Europe-wide delivery.

Do you deliver to PO boxes?

No. Every delivery needs a business address.

Is the shipment handed over in person?

Yes, if you request it. The driver hands over to the named recipient, who signs for receipt. See Personal handover.

Do you collect in my town?

We operate across the whole Rhine-Neckar metropolitan region, for example in Mannheim, Ludwigshafen, Heidelberg, Speyer, Landau and Karlsruhe.
